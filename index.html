What we do

Support that keeps children learning

Three simple programmes that remove the everyday barriers between a child and the classroom.

School fees

We cover fees for children whose families can't, so they can enrol and stay in school all year.

Books & supplies

Every child gets a kit of books, stationery and a uniform at the start of the school year.

After-school tutoring

Volunteer tutors help children catch up in reading and maths in small weekend groups.

Why we are here

Education should open doors for every child.

Every child has potential. Too often, a missing fee or an empty school bag is all that stands in the way. EduKind steps in with practical help and steady encouragement.

  • Support planned around each child's needs
  • Close work with families, schools and volunteers
  • Clear reporting on where every gift goes
