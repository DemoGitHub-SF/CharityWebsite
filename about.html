Our story

Every child deserves the opportunity to learn.

Edukind is built around a simple belief: education can create choices, build confidence and help communities flourish. We want a world where a child's opportunity to learn is not limited by their circumstances.

By bringing people together around children's education, we aim to help create more inclusive, supportive environments where young people can explore their interests and imagine new possibilities.
