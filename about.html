Our story

Every child deserves the opportunity to learn.

EduKind is built on a simple belief: education creates choices, builds confidence and helps whole communities flourish. A child's chance to learn should never depend on their circumstances.

We bring donors, volunteers, families and schools together around one goal: keeping children in school and helping them thrive there.
